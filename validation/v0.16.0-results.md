# v0.16.0 validation

Validated offline with Zeek 9.0.1 and generated packet captures. **Zeek 8.x and
the Corelight sensor have not been tested yet**; the next test is a rebuilt bundle
on the AP200.

- `python3 tests/run_tests.py`: every synthetic suite passes. The optional UVic
  and kresoin real-capture fixtures were not present locally and were skipped.
- `tests/test_wifi.py` (rewritten for the inventory, 17 scenarios):
  - a deauthentication burst on a tracked drone AP is a prompt `manipulation`
    finding on that AP's single record, and its counts restart after each record;
  - a disassociation burst produces its own finding;
  - drone APs matched by SSID pattern and by drone-vendor OUI are both covered;
  - **non-drone networks with deauthentication and disassociation bursts produce
    no `drone.log` records**;
  - negatives: under threshold, AP never seen, retry, protected, duplicate,
    mixed types, handshake-only, slow, and bad-FCS frames;
  - EAPOL counted as context on a later minute-limited update;
  - threshold tuning and the `DroneRadio::enabled` off switch.
- `tests/test_logging_policy.py`: a Tello AP's burst now yields
  `detection`, `manipulation`, `final` on one record, and disconnect counts across
  those records sum to the 25 frames sent.
- `tests/test_wireless.py`: 13 inventory/Remote ID/GRE scenarios pass with
  production logging defaults. They previously failed under the harness's legacy
  settings, which suppress the final record that carries inventory totals.
- SSID matching: `DJI-Mavic-123`, `Skydio-1` and `Anafi-4K` are now recognized;
  `ordinary-wifi`, `DJIfan` and `xTELLO-58ECCC` are not.
- Loaded together with [wifi-telemetry](https://github.com/zeekallab/wifi-telemetry)
  v0.3.0, in either load order: `drone.log` contained only the drone AP and its
  finding, while `wifi.log`/`wifi_findings.log` covered both the drone and the
  ordinary network.

Before this release every test failed at its first Zeek run, because the one-time
`drone-detector:` INFO diagnostics added in v0.15.0 appear in `reporter.log`. The
harness now ignores only those diagnostics.
