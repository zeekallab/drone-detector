# v0.8.0 validation

Validated offline on Zeek 9.0.1 on 2026-11-03. No Corelight deployment or live
performance test was performed.

**186 synthetic cases passed**: the prior 166 cases plus 20 video scenarios.
Video checks cover recent SDK pair identity, incorrect direction/port, absent or
stale state, missing SPS/PPS/IDR, insufficient distinct slices, invalid header
bits/profile, expired evidence, 3-/4-byte start codes, combined NAL prefixes,
minute gating, inspection budget renewal, prefix bounds and streamon-only negatives.
Synthetic packets have valid transport checksums; tests use the actual Zeek runtime.

The original DJI capture, kresoin MAVLink capture and four existing UVic fixture
checks also pass. The original DJI regression retains 2,769 valid messages and
1,220 matched responses; the MAVLink fixture retains its telemetry validation.

## Selected video captures

Only two of the seven Video files were replayed. Default package loading enables
the new module; optional ARP/Wi-Fi modules were not loaded.

| Capture | All drone.log rows | Video rows | Video connections | Inspection limited |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| video_6_5_14_5mins.pcap | 18 | 6 | 1 | false |
| video_7_5_14_10mins.pcap | 30 | 10 | 1 | false |

Both identify source 192.168.10.1 and receiver 192.168.10.4, with
`protocol=tello-video`, `video_active=true` and `video_codec=H264`. Video presence
adds no attack alert. Every connection's successive records are at least 60
seconds apart and no reporter errors were produced. Multiple protocol connections
explain why total file rows exceed the video-row count.

Independent ffprobe inspection of bounded locally extracted samples identified
H.264 Main profile at 960×720 in both. Midstream/truncation diagnostics were
present; this validates codec metadata, not flawless decoding of the entire
capture. The Zeek module performs bounded structural recognition rather than
full decoding. Neither samples nor footage are committed.

## Reproduction

```sh
DJI_CAPTURE=/path/to/djiuav.pcap.gz python3 tests/run_tests.py
ISOT_CAPTURE_ROOT='/path/to/ISOT Drone Dataset/Raw Data' python3 tests/test_video_captures.py
```

Optional real fixtures report SKIP when absent. Real video replays use `-C` to
inspect payloads independently of recorded transport checksums. This does not
authenticate media or disable existing binary Tello application CRC checks.
[Member hashes](../tests/fixtures/uvic/video-manifest.json) identify tested originals.

## Scope

Coverage requires the same worker to see recent valid SDK state and the supported
raw H.264 transport. Generic cameras without that context do not match. Missing,
encrypted, differently encapsulated, cross-datagram or out-of-prefix evidence can
be missed. Crafted/replayed evidence can imitate protocol structure. No universal
false-positive rate or support for all FPV drones is claimed. A positive record
shows transmitted video at that timestamp, not viewer identity, piloting mode,
physical movement, or persistent status. No stop event or session-end flush is
emitted, and no DoS detector was added.
