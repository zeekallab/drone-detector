# v0.5.0 validation

Validated offline on Zeek 9.0.1 on 2026-11-03. No sensor deployment or production
load test was performed.

## Automated regression results

**125 synthetic scenarios passed**: 12 legacy DJI, 28 MAVLink framing,
21 telemetry/logging, 13 control/anomaly, 20 Tello SDK, 19 binary Tello and
12 optional wireless scenarios. Coverage includes CRC failures, direction and
port scope, replay age/diversity/fraction thresholds, malformed-control
confirmation, bounded tracking, mixed-protocol minute gating, benign handshakes,
retry filtering, bad-FCS/protected wireless frames and deduplication.

The original DJI capture still yields 2,769 valid messages and 1,220 matched
responses. The kresoin capture still yields two connections and four rate-limited
records with telemetry. Four existing UVic fixture checks also pass: nine SDK
records in the injection fixture, plus binary identification, and identification
of the three binary fixtures without false MAVLink/legacy-DJI classification.

## Limits

Three normal flights are a limited comparison, not a measured general false-positive
rate. CRC is integrity evidence, not sender authentication. The replay rule misses
some rapid, single-payload or modified replays. Bounds can omit evidence; limit
flags must be considered. No session-end flush means brief late activity may go
unreported. Wi-Fi support requires raw radiotap input and explicit loading;
Ethernet mirrors cannot supply its evidence. Offline cracking and successful
takeover are not established by these alerts.
